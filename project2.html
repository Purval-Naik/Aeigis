What it actually does

Three parts, built to work without needing a live backend or a trained model.

01
Message scanner
Paste any message. Aegis checks it against a growing rulebook of real scam patterns — fake authority, false urgency, money requests, suspicious links — and scores the risk.
02
Plain-language explain
Every flag comes with a one-line reason in ordinary words, not jargon. The goal is understanding, not just a red or green light.
03
Awareness quiz
Real-style scam and safe messages, side by side. Users guess, then see why they were right or wrong — the same message could show up in their own inbox tomorrow.
